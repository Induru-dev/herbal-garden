Curry Leaf

Murraya koenigii (L.) Spreng.

  • Local nameKarapincha (කරපිංචා)
  • ScientificMurraya koenigii (L.) Spreng.
  • FamilyRutaceae (Citrus family)
  • Plant habitSmall aromatic shrub or tree

About this plant

Karapincha is a small aromatic shrub or tree whose leaves are an essential part of Sri Lankan cooking. It is native to Sri Lanka and India.

How to identify it

  • A small aromatic shrub or tree.
  • Compound leaves made up of many small, glossy, dark green, pointed leaflets arranged along a central stem.
  • Clusters of small white flowers.
  • Small berries that turn from green to black when ripe.

Traditional uses and importance

  • Fresh leaves are used to flavour curries, sambols and herbal porridges (kola kenda).
  • In traditional Ayurvedic medicine, the leaves are valued for supporting digestion.
  • Traditionally used to reduce inflammation and to promote healthy hair and skin.

Garden notes

Murraya koenigii thrives in sunny locations with well-drained soil. Regular harvesting of the leaf stems encourages bushy growth, and it can be propagated using fresh seeds or root suckers.
