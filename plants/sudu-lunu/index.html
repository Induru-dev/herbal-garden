Garlic

Allium sativum L.
සුදු ළූණු
  • Local nameSudu Lunu (සුදු ළූණු)
  • ScientificAllium sativum L.
  • FamilyAmaryllidaceae
  • Plant habitPerennial bulbous herb, usually cultivated as an annual

About this plant

Sudu Lunu is a bulbous plant that grows underground. It is widely used around the world and in Sri Lanka for cooking, and it is also an important medicinal herb in traditional healthcare systems.

How to identify it

  • Usually grows about 45 to 60 cm tall.
  • Has a segmented, white underground bulb made up of several cloves.
  • Leaves are long, flat and narrow, without distinct leaf blades.
  • Produces small whitish or pinkish flowers arranged in an umbel cluster.

Traditional uses and importance

  • In traditional medicine and Ayurveda, garlic bulbs and extracts are associated with managing high blood pressure and supporting heart health.
  • Traditionally used to help reduce joint pain.
  • Used as a natural remedy for various inflammatory conditions.

Safety note

Garlic is a common food ingredient, but concentrated extracts or large medicinal amounts should be used with caution. Seek professional advice if you use it alongside other medications.
