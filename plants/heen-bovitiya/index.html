Endemic Medicinal Shrub

Heen Bovitiya

Osbeckia octandra (L.) DC.

About this plant

Heen Bovitiya (eight stamen osbeckia) is a medicinal shrub endemic to Sri Lanka, meaning it grows naturally nowhere else. It is the provincial flower of the Southern Province.

How to identify it

  • A small, branched evergreen shrub growing up to 2 m tall.
  • Reddish-brown, hairy stems.
  • Simple, narrow green leaves with three distinct lengthwise veins.
  • Striking pinkish-purple flowers with five petals and bright yellow centres.

Traditional uses and importance

  • In Sri Lankan traditional and Ayurvedic medicine, the leaves, stems and roots are widely used in preparations for liver disorders such as jaundice.
  • Young leaves are prepared as herbal porridge (kola kenda) or herbal tea.
  • Traditionally associated with supporting liver health and managing diabetes.

Garden notes

Heen Bovitiya grows well in moist, well-drained soil in sunny or partially shaded areas. Its flowers attract bees and butterflies for pollination.

Safety note

Traditional uses do not prove that a plant can treat disease. Anyone with a liver condition or diabetes should follow their doctor's advice.
