Spice Tree

Nutmeg

Myristica fragrans Houtt.
සාදික්කා
  • Local nameSadikka (සාදික්කා)
  • ScientificMyristica fragrans Houtt.
  • FamilyMyristicaceae
  • Plant habitPerennial evergreen tree

About this plant

Nutmeg is a medium-sized, aromatic evergreen tree native to the Banda Islands (Maluku) of Indonesia. A single fruit gives two spices: nutmeg from the seed, and mace from the red, net-like covering around the seed. In Sri Lanka it is mainly grown in the Kandy, Matale and Kegalle districts.

How to identify it

  • Usually grows about 5 to 15 m tall, occasionally up to 20 m.
  • A spreading crown with many branches.
  • Greyish-brown bark; young branches are usually green.
  • Simple, alternate, oval or lance-shaped leaves with smooth edges.
  • Leaves are dark green and shiny above, lighter underneath, and aromatic when crushed.
  • Small, pale yellow, bell-shaped flowers.
  • Oval or pear-shaped fruits that turn yellow when mature.

Traditional uses and importance

  • The dried seed is used as a spice in curries, baked goods, sweets and drinks.
  • Mace is used as a flavouring in many foods.
  • Essential oils from nutmeg and mace are used for flavouring and fragrance.
  • Recorded in traditional Ayurvedic and Chinese medicine.
  • An important part of Sri Lanka's spice industry and exports.

Flowers and fruits

Male and female flowers usually grow on separate trees. Female flowers develop into fleshy yellow fruits. When ripe, the fruit splits into two halves, revealing a shiny dark brown seed wrapped in a bright red, lacy covering called the aril. The dried seed kernel is nutmeg, and the dried aril is mace.

Garden notes

Nutmeg grows well in warm, humid tropical climates in well-drained, fertile soil rich in organic matter. Young trees need shade, and trees only begin to flower and fruit after several years. It is a good plant for studying spice crops, seed development and the economic value of plants.

Safety note

Nutmeg is meant for use as a spice in normal cooking amounts. Eating large amounts can cause serious health effects, so it should never be used in concentrated amounts or as a home remedy.
