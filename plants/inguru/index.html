Ginger

Zingiber officinale Roscoe
ඉඟුරු
  • Local nameInguru (ඉඟුරු)
  • ScientificZingiber officinale Roscoe
  • FamilyZingiberaceae (Ginger family)
  • Plant habitPerennial herb growing from a rhizome

About this plant

Ginger is a tropical herb widely cultivated in Sri Lanka and other Asian countries. It is commonly used as a spice in cooking because of its unique aroma and spicy flavour. The underground rhizome is the main part used.

How to identify it

  • Grows from an underground rhizome and produces leafy shoots above the ground.
  • Long, narrow, green leaves arranged alternately along the stem.
  • The rhizome is light brown on the outside and yellowish inside.
  • It has a strong, characteristic aroma and a spicy taste.

Traditional uses and importance

  • Commonly used as a spice in food preparation.
  • Adds flavour and aroma to foods and beverages.
  • Traditionally used in herbal preparations.
  • Contains natural compounds such as gingerol.

Garden notes

Ginger is a perennial herb that grows well in warm and humid tropical climates. It is commonly cultivated in home gardens and agricultural lands, and the rhizome is harvested for culinary and traditional uses. Ginger is an important example of a plant used in food culture and traditional practices.
