Pandan

Pandanus amaryllifolius Roxb.
රම්පේ
  • Local nameRampe (රම්පේ)
  • ScientificPandanus amaryllifolius Roxb.
  • FamilyPandanaceae (Screwpine family)
  • Plant habitUpright perennial herb

About this plant

Rampe is an upright tropical plant grown in almost every Sri Lankan home garden for its sweet-smelling leaves. A few leaves added to rice, curries or sweets give them a pleasant aroma.

How to identify it

  • An upright plant with long, narrow, strap-like green leaves arranged in a spiral.
  • The leaves are smooth, without sharp spines on the edges.
  • The leaves release a sweet, pleasant aroma when crushed or torn.
  • Older plants develop woody aerial roots that help support the stem.

Traditional uses and importance

  • The fragrant leaves are widely used in Sri Lankan and Asian cooking to add aroma and flavour to rice, curries and sweets.
  • In traditional practices, leaf infusions are used as cooling herbal drinks.
  • Traditionally used in natural remedies to aid digestion.

Garden notes

Pandanus amaryllifolius rarely produces flowers or seeds and is easily grown from small side shoots (suckers) taken from the base of a mature plant. It grows well in warm, moist soil.
