Mukunuwenna

Alternanthera sessilis (L.) R.Br. ex DC.
මුගුණුවැන්න
  • Local nameMukunuwenna (මුගුණුවැන්න)
  • ScientificAlternanthera sessilis (L.) R.Br. ex DC.
  • FamilyAmaranthaceae (Amaranth family)
  • Plant habitLow-growing, spreading perennial herb

About this plant

Mukunuwenna (sessile joyweed) is one of the most popular leafy vegetables in Sri Lanka. It is a low, spreading herb that grows quickly in damp places.

How to identify it

  • A low-growing, spreading perennial herb.
  • Creeping stems that root at the nodes.
  • Small, narrow, opposite green leaves.
  • Tiny, rounded clusters of silvery-white flowers directly in the leaf axils.

Traditional uses and importance

  • Commonly eaten shredded with grated coconut (mallung) or used in herbal porridges.
  • In traditional medicine it is valued for its cooling properties.
  • Traditionally associated with good eyesight, digestion, and healthy hair and skin.

Garden notes

Alternanthera sessilis thrives in damp, fertile soils and grows rapidly in home gardens. It is very easy to propagate using stem cuttings and can be harvested again and again by trimming the tender top shoots.
