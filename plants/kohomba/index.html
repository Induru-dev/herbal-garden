Neem

About this plant

Neem is a fast-growing evergreen tree widely found in tropical and subtropical regions. It is known for its bitter leaves, small white flowers and yellow fruits, and it is valued for its traditional uses, environmental importance and role in natural pest management.

How to identify it

  • A medium to large tree with spreading branches.
  • Compound leaves made of several small, serrated leaflets arranged along a central stalk.
  • Small, fragrant white flowers in clusters.
  • Oval-shaped fruits that turn yellow when ripe.

Traditional uses and importance

  • Leaves, bark, seeds and oil are all used in traditional practices.
  • Neem leaves are used in some traditional household preparations.
  • Neem seed oil is used in certain agricultural pest management products.
  • Neem contains natural compounds that are important in botanical pest control.

Garden notes

Neem grows well in warm tropical climates. It prefers sunny locations and can tolerate relatively dry conditions once established. The tree provides shade and supports biodiversity, and it is important in sustainable agriculture because compounds from its seeds are used in botanical pesticides.

Safety note

Neem leaves and other plant parts should not be considered automatically safe to eat. Neem oil and concentrated neem preparations may be harmful if swallowed or misused. Follow appropriate safety precautions when handling neem products.
