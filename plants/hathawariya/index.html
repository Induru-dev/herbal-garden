Hathawariya (Wild Asparagus)

About this plant

Hathawariya is a perennial, climbing and bushy medicinal plant. Its thick tuberous roots are the most valued part of the plant in traditional medicine.

How to identify it

  • A perennial, climbing and bushy plant.
  • Stems are slender, woody and branched, with small sharp spines.
  • Leaves are very small and needle-like.
  • Flowers are small, white and fragrant.
  • Fruits are small, round berries.
  • Roots are thick, fleshy and tuberous.

Traditional uses and importance

  • Roots are traditionally used in Ayurvedic medicine.
  • Traditionally used as a herbal tonic.
  • Traditionally associated with digestive health.
  • Traditionally used in preparations related to women's health.
  • An important medicinal plant in traditional medicine.

Garden notes

Hathawariya grows as a climber. It prefers warm conditions and well-drained soil, and it can be propagated by seeds and root divisions. Its tuberous roots are an important part of the plant.

Safety note

These descriptions explain traditional uses, not proven medical treatments. Do not prepare or consume medicinal remedies without qualified advice.
