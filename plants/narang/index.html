About this plant

Narang is a small evergreen fruit tree widely grown in tropical and subtropical regions. It produces round, orange fruits with a sweet or slightly sour taste. The fruits are rich in vitamin C and are commonly eaten fresh or used in juices and food.

How to identify it

  • A small to medium-sized evergreen tree.
  • Green, aromatic leaves, generally oval with pointed tips.
  • Small, white, fragrant flowers.
  • Round fruits that turn orange when ripe.
  • The fruit has a loose peel that comes off easily.
  • Juicy segments inside, with seeds in some varieties.

Traditional uses and importance

  • The fruit is commonly eaten fresh.
  • Used to prepare fresh juices and beverages.
  • Provides vitamin C, which supports normal immune function.
  • The peel is traditionally used in some herbal preparations.
  • The aromatic leaves and peel are used in traditional practices.
  • Used in cooking to add flavour and fragrance.

Garden notes

Narang grows well in warm climates with plenty of sunlight and well-drained soil. Its fragrant flowers attract pollinating insects. It is a good plant for teaching visitors about fruit production, citrus plants and nutrition.

Safety note

Wash the fruits thoroughly before eating. People with citrus allergies should avoid them. Traditional medicinal uses of the peel and leaves should not be considered proven medical treatments.
