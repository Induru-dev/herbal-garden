Komarika (Aloe Vera)

Aloe vera (L.) Burm.f.
කෝමාරිකා
  • Local nameKomarika (කෝමාරිකා)
  • ScientificAloe vera (L.) Burm.f.
  • FamilyAsphodelaceae
  • Plant habitPerennial succulent herb

About this plant

Komarika is a short, green succulent that stores water in its thick leaves. It is one of the best known medicinal plants and is easy to grow even in dry areas.

How to identify it

  • A short, green, succulent plant.
  • Leaves are thick, fleshy and arranged in a rosette.
  • Leaves have sharp teeth along their margins.
  • Leaves contain a clear, jelly-like gel inside.
  • Flowers are yellow and tubular.
  • It can grow in dry areas with little water.

Traditional uses and importance

  • The gel is traditionally used for minor burns and skin care.
  • Commonly used in cosmetics, shampoos and skin-care products.
  • Traditionally used in some herbal preparations.
  • An important medicinal plant in traditional medicine.

Garden notes

Komarika is an easy-to-grow medicinal plant. It needs well-drained soil and moderate watering, and it can be propagated using the young plantlets that grow around the mother plant.

Safety note

These descriptions explain traditional uses, not proven medical treatments. Do not prepare or consume medicinal remedies without qualified advice.
